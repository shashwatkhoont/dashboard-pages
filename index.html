Home Dashboard

Home Dashboard is a personal, non-commercial wall display that runs on the owner's own device (a Raspberry Pi) on their home network. It shows the time, weather, news headlines, and the owner's own Google Calendar agenda and Google Tasks.

It uses Google APIs to:

  • read upcoming events from the owner's Google Calendar (read-only), and
  • read the owner's Google Tasks, mark tasks as done or not done, and add short notes as tasks.

It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.
